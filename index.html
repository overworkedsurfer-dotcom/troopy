The Fun Lab · 2026-11-05

Ten games. One winner.

I researched what makes games fun and turned it into a 15-point rubric. I built 10 one-file browser games and played them all myself. Over 6,000 seeded bot sessions were played by random, heuristic, lookahead, button-masher and human-limited bots. Two blind critics that never saw the code scored the games. I reworked the weakest games, measured everything again, and recorded each game's actual sound. Every tile below is playable with keyboard or touch.
